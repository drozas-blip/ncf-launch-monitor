Week over week — the journey, step by step

i

Each cell counts what happened that day — not a cohort. Someone who became a lead last week can book this week, so a row's later columns aren't produced by that row's leads. Conversion % is shown only up to Trials — those steps happen in the same session, so a weekly ratio holds. Booked · Attended · Rx lag by days, so this week's attendees were booked in an earlier week: a within-week ratio there would read over 100% and mean nothing.

Website ≠ the top of this funnel. It counts visits to the home page (findbalance.app / metodobalance.es) — total traffic, not new-flow only, since that event carries no flow tag. Most paid traffic goes straight to the form at /evaluacion, skipping the home page, which is why form opens can exceed website visits. Read them as two separate front doors, not one funnel.

Baseline & caveats

  • Old = the comparison baseline is May–June 2026 — the old flow's steady state before the 20 Jul cutover. New = since launch (20 Jul).
  • Booking & app rates are matched within 2 days of signup, so account maturity doesn't skew the comparison.
  • Test users are excluded in the DB (email/name contains test/nexu). Mixpanel events can't be test-filtered (small share).
  • There is no "payment-page viewed" event — the checkout funnel isn't measurable per flow (Stripe only shows completed charges).
  • Channel attribution (paid vs organic) is empty in the data.
